Deployment Guide

🔥 Step 1: Deploy on TRON (TRC20)

  1. Open tronscan.org → Contracts → Deploy
  2. Connect TronLink wallet with TRX for gas fees (~50 TRX per contract)
  3. Upload TUCB_All_12_TRC20.sol from the contracts folder
  4. Compile with Solidity 0.8.0
  5. Deploy TUCBTokenFactory → call deployAll() → all 12 tokens live
  6. Verify contracts on Tronscan for public transparency

Total gas cost: ~600 TRX (~$60) for all 12 contracts · LOLY code-gate embedded in every contract

⟠ Step 2: Deploy on Ethereum (ERC20)

  1. Open Remix IDE
  2. Upload ERC20 contract templates
  3. Connect MetaMask with ETH for gas
  4. Deploy each contract individually
  5. Add to Uniswap for DEX trading

Gas cost: ~$200-500 per contract (varies with network)

🟡 Step 3: Deploy on BSC (BEP20)

  1. Open Remix IDE → switch to BSC network
  2. Upload BEP20 contract templates
  3. Connect MetaMask with BNB for gas
  4. Deploy contracts
  5. Create liquidity pools on PancakeSwap

Gas cost: ~$10-20 per contract (lowest cost option)
